About Sunwai

Sunwai is a privacy-preserving public-health accountability platform where verified residents can anonymously report recurring problems at public health facilities, trigger a community-level case, and verify whether the issue was actually resolved.

Our Mission

To empower communities to hold public health facilities accountable while protecting citizen privacy through cryptographic verification.

Key Features

  • Anonymous reporting with zero-identity disclosure
  • Community-triggered accountability cases
  • Official response tracking
  • Resident verification before resolution
  • Escalation when deadlines expire
  • Impact analytics and pattern detection

Technology

Built with privacy-preserving technology, community verification, and transparent case tracking.

Core message: VERIFY → REPORT ANONYMOUSLY → REACH THRESHOLD → OFFICIAL RESPONDS → COMMUNITY VERIFIES → RESOLVE OR ESCALATE
